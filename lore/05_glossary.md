# Glossary

Terms as the game uses them. "In-world" is what a resident would say; where it is blank, the plain word is used.

| Game term | In-world | Meaning | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Solidification | The Stilling | The phenomenon that turns women to stone | Term fixed; in-world name proposed |
| Gauge, "stone" | The creep | How far the stone has taken someone, 0 to 100 | Proposed |
| Statue | The stilled | A fully solidified person who may still be restored | Proposed |
| Permanent | The kept | A statue abandoned for thirty days; beyond help | Proposed |
| Sanctuary | | The last protected settlement | Fixed |
| Goddess | | Its protector | Fixed; unnamed |
| Miracle | The turning | Her one great intervention when the frontier falls | Proposed |
| Summoning seal | The seal | The circle in the guild hall that calls champions | Fixed |
| Adventurer, champion | Champion | A woman who answered the seal | Fixed |
| Emergency summon | A borrowed hand | A champion called in haste who fades after two weeks | Proposed |
| Guild master | Summoner | The player | Fixed |
| Residents | | The surviving people of this world | Fixed |
| Labyrinth | The deep | The buried layers below | Proposed |
| Stratum | A layer | One themed section of the labyrinth, a few floors deep | Fixed |
| Frontier | The line | The guild's forward defensive position | Fixed |
| Invasion | The surge | The weekly attack on the frontier | Proposed |
| Ward energy | Ward-light | Stored protection against the Stilling | Fixed |
| Construction materials | Stone and timber | What repairs and builds | Fixed |
| Restoration reagent | Green | What the physician uses to bring a statue back | Proposed |
| Recovery ward | The ward | Where statues are restored | Fixed |
| Expedition | A descent | A party's trip into the labyrinth | Proposed |
| Elite | A walker | A powerful creature that patrols a fixed route | Proposed |
| Boss | A master | The creature that rules a stratum | Proposed |
| Watchtower, bunker, support station | Tower, hold, lamp-post | The three defensive structures | Proposed |
