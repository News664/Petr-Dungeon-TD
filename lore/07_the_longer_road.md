# The longer road

**Everything here is Proposed** unless marked. It answers the open question of where the Keeper's charge leads.

**Fixed.** The Keeper of the Galleries guarded a statue Iwanaga wants for good. Bringing it home unlocks stories from Sakuya and can open a longer, harder line of quests.

**In the game now.** The statue is the first champion Sakuya ever called and the first Iwanaga took. Carrying her to the Still Grove unlocks "The first to answer". Nothing follows yet.

## The idea

The first to answer cannot be restored. She has been permanent for longer than anyone remembers. But she left things behind, in every stratum, because she walked them all before the Stilling closed over them: the first champion was also the first delver. The longer road follows her steps down.

It is a quest line that runs alongside the main campaign, never required, always harder, and it pays in story and in a few things that matter, never in a way to undo the Stilling.

## Steps

| # | Where | What is found | What it costs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pale Galleries, floor 1 | Her travelling pack, in the locked store, under the cache nobody looked beneath. A name stitched inside it. Sakuya says the name aloud for the first time in a hundred years. | Nothing: it is the invitation. |
| 2 | Drowned Cloister, floor 2 | Her letters to Sakuya, in the refectory behind the water, kept dry in a sealed jar by someone who could not read them. They tell where she went next. | Reaching the refectory at low tide with a carrying place free, past the Bellringer's ring. |
| 3 | Drowned Cloister, floor 3 | The door she marked, under the ice. Only open at low tide and only reachable by sliding from the right place. | A hard optional fight: the Abbess's first novice, who knew her. |
| 4 | Gilded Exchange | Her page in the ledgers: what Iwanaga paid for her. It is not a price. It is a promise, and the guild can read it. | A trade with the Assayer that costs something real. |
| 5 | Below the Exchange | Where she stood when she was taken. What she was trying to reach. | Unknown. This is the edge of what is written. |

## What it gives

- Story: the clearest picture anyone has of what Iwanaga is and why she began.
- Small, permanent help that is not power: a second carrying place for one champion; the tide table of the Cloister written on the guild's chart; the Assayer's ledger telling where a missing champion's statue lies.
- Never: a way to restore a permanent statue, a way to stop the clock, or anything that makes solidification useful.

## Open

- Her name.
- Whether she can be heard: a voice in the grove, in dreams, in the seal.
- What waits at step 5, and whether it changes the ending.
