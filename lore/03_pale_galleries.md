# The first stratum: the Pale Galleries

## What it was

**Proposed.** The nearest buried layer, and the most recent. Within living memory it was the pride of the hill country: long halls where the region's painters, weavers and sculptors showed their work, with cloisters for teaching and a great court for festivals. When the Stilling rose through it, it took the colour first. Every canvas is blank pale stone now, every tapestry a slab, and the visitors are still standing in front of them.

The name is what the residents call it. Its old name is lost. **Open.**

## The floors

**Fixed.** What each floor is for:

| Floor | Purpose |
|---|---|
| 1 | The basics, in several distinct areas |
| 2 | The roaming elite, and the first traps |
| 3 | The first permanent statues, used as decoration and as parts of the floor's mechanisms, to show what the master of the stratum can do |

Much more is wanted on every floor than the prototype has.

**In the game now:**

| Floor | Name | What is there |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Outer Galleries | The entrance halls and the barred service gate; the storeroom to work for timber and stone; a drift of pale mist. East, the restorers' wing: a key left on a workbench, and a locked store nobody has opened since the fall. |
| 2 | Gallery of Echoes | The ring of cloisters the Warden walks, around the hall where a herb garden still grows. Two pressure plates that loose pale dust. And one wall on the east side that is not a wall. |
| 3 | The Pale Court | The festival court. An exhibition hall where the exhibits are people. One of them holds the short door to the sculptors' yard open with her raised arms. One stands alone in a side room, too easy to reach. |

**Proposed, not yet built:** distinct creatures for each area of floor 1; a corridor on floor 2 that closes behind the party; a statue on floor 3 that points the wrong way.

## What lives there

| Creature | What it is |
|---|---|
| Pale Crawler | A scavenger that used to live under the floors and now lives on them. Eats mortar. |
| Dust Moth | Feeds on the pale dust and sheds it. Where they gather the stone creeps faster. |
| Stone Hound | Something the Stilling made out of the watch-dogs of the galleries, or made to look like them. |
| Basalt Sentinel | A plinth that got up. Slow, patient, braces before it strikes. |
| Gazer | An eye in a shell. It is not angry. Looking is all it does, and being looked at by it is enough. |

**Fixed (by the rules).** Dust Moths, Gazers and both great creatures here solidify. Their shared sign is a cold white-violet light.

## The Warden of Dust

**Proposed.** The last keeper of the galleries walked her rounds every night. Something still does. It is dust and scraps of ceremonial armour around an empty helm, and it follows her route around the cloister ring exactly, at her pace, forever. It does not hunt. It will not leave the ring. It destroys whatever is in its path.

A party can time its passage and never fight it. Killing it is a choice, and it comes back within a week: the dust gathers again.

**Fixed.** The keeper can be found. She is a hidden boss, reached only through hidden quests or hidden shortcuts, and she is harder and trickier than the Colossus.

**Proposed.** The Warden is her route without her. She is elsewhere, behind a wall the ring does not show, still keeping the one room Iwanaga never entered. She is not stone and has not been for a long time; what she is instead is the question of the fight.

**In the game now.** A ring of keys lies in the baited room on floor 3. With it, a wall on floor 2 opens onto her rooms. She fights by rearranging: the party's front and back rows change places.

**Open.** What she guards, and what she says.

## The Hollow Colossus

**Proposed.** The master of the stratum is a statue. It was the centrepiece of the sculptors' yard, unfinished: a great seated figure meant to honour the hill country's founders, hollow inside to save stone. The Stilling filled the hollow. It draws breath now, slowly, and what it breathes out is the pale wind that solidifies a whole party at once.

When it falls, the wind stops. The monsters of the stratum lose whatever was holding them to a purpose and scatter into the cracks. The air is still dangerous, but it is only air.

## What the guild learns here

**Proposed.** Each floor should give the player one true thing about the catastrophe. Suggestions:

1. **Outer Galleries.** The Stilling took colour and cloth before it took people. It works on made things as well as living ones. (A resident recognises a blank frame as her mother's painting.)
2. **Gallery of Echoes.** Something living survived untouched in the sealed hall: plants. It is not life the Stilling hates. This is where restoration reagent comes from.
3. **The Pale Court.** The Colossus was hollow, and the Stilling pooled in it. It gathers in empty, enclosed places. It behaves like something that flows, and it flowed up from below.

**Open.** Whether these are the right three, and what the party carries away that points to the second stratum.

## After it is secured

**Fixed.** Workers move in behind wards. The stratum produces ward energy, materials and reagent every day while its upkeep is paid and the frontier in front of it holds.

**Proposed.** The residents do not strip it. They set the wards, then they start, carefully, bringing the statues of the gallery visitors up to the sanctuary's memorial, though every one of them is decades past saving. Nobody ordered this.
