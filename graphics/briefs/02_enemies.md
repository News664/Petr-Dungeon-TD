# Enemies

## What these pictures are for

- **Labyrinth monsters** appear in the turn-based fights. Today they are text cards; each will become a picture above its health bar. They are seen from the front, at about 300 px on a desktop screen.
- **Defense-battle monsters** walk the lanes of the battle maps. They are small (about 20–40 px on screen) and seen from above at an angle, so silhouette and one accent colour matter far more than detail.

## Must be true

- Not people, and not shaped like people. They are of the stone world, which is why the stone does not take them.
- Pale, dusty, mineral; one accent colour each.
- The three that solidify their victims (Dust Moth, Gazer, and the Petrifier of the defense maps) and the two large ones that can (Warden of Dust, Hollow Colossus) share the cold white-violet glow. A player should learn to fear that colour.
- Transparent background. No ground shadow.
- The elite and the boss should feel larger than the frame: let the Colossus crowd its canvas.
