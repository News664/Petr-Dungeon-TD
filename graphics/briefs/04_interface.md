# Interface

## Icons

The interface is text-heavy and dark. Icons mark the things a player scans for: her class, the three resources, the stone gauge, the miracle.

| Icon | Meaning | Colour |
|---|---|---|
| Class: Guardian, Gunner, Alchemist, Medic | Shown beside every adventurer | Steel blue, amber, violet, teal |
| Ward energy | Protective power; pays for wards, recalls and summons | Teal |
| Construction materials | Repairs, rebuilding, equipment | Warm grey |
| Restoration reagent | Restoring statues, supplies | Soft green |
| Stone | The solidification gauge | Pale grey |
| Guard | A guarding adventurer in a fight | Parchment |
| Miracle | Sakuya's one charge | Blossom pink |

- 256 × 256 px, transparent background, a single centred symbol.
- Two tones at most. They are shown at 16–32 px, so no fine detail and no outlines thinner than a twelfth of the canvas.
- The class icons replace the plain emblems the game draws today; keep the same ideas (shield, crosshair, flask, cross) so nothing has to be relearned.
